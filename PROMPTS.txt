I want to build a production-quality, multi-tenant SaaS application for catering businesses.

The application will include:
- A Super Admin Console to track all registered catering businesses (tenants).
- Tenant-specific business administration and user roles.
- Customer and enquiry management.
- Food catalogue and menu builder.
- Quotations, quotation revisions and booking confirmation.
- WhatsApp, email and SMS integrations.
- Modules for invoices, payments, daily meal orders, inventory, procurement and kitchen operations.

## Technology stack

- Frontend: Next.js, React and TypeScript.
- Styling: Tailwind CSS and daisyUI.
- Forms: Tanstack Form.
- Validation: Zod.
- Server data: Tanstack Query.
- Tables: Tanstack Table.
- Backend: NestJS and TypeScript.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- ORM: Prisma.
- Testing: Playwright plus appropriate unit and integration testing tools.
