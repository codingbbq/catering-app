# Product Vision

## Vision statement

Build the operating system for modern catering businesses: a multi-tenant SaaS platform that helps teams move from lead capture to profitable fulfillment with better pricing discipline, faster response times, and clearer operational control.

## Who the product serves

- Event catering businesses
- Caterers offering recurring corporate or institutional meals
- Catering businesses that mix event-driven and recurring meal services
- The platform operator that onboards and governs tenant businesses

## Product promise

For tenant businesses, the platform centralizes sales, pricing, booking, communication, and later operations in one tenant-isolated system.

For the SaaS operator, the platform provides a controlled Super Admin Console for tenant lifecycle management, platform support, and compliance oversight.

## Strategic principles

- Sales-first delivery: get quoting and booking workflows working before deep operational modules.
- Tenant isolation first: platform growth must not compromise data segregation.
- Modular growth: add finance and operations after the commercial workflow is stable.
- Shared contracts: align request and response contracts with reusable schemas where practical.
- Practical UX: optimize for repetitive business tasks, not generic admin screens.

## Desired business outcomes

- Faster enquiry response and quotation turnaround
- Better gross margin visibility at quote time
- Higher conversion from enquiry to booking
- Reduced operational errors caused by disconnected records
- Scalable onboarding of new tenant businesses

## Product pillars

### Pillar 1: Platform trust

- Secure tenancy model
- Reliable role-based access
- Platform-level supervision through the Super Admin Console

### Pillar 2: Commercial workflow excellence

- Clean CRM and enquiry intake
- Reusable catalogue and menu composition
- Accurate costing and revisioned quotations
- Smooth conversion from quotation to booking

### Pillar 3: Operational expansion

- Finance workflows
- Daily meal fulfillment
- Inventory, procurement, and kitchen planning
- Reporting and analytics

## Pilot release vision

The first pilot should enable a small catering business to:

- onboard as a tenant,
- invite tenant users,
- manage customers and enquiries,
- build menus from a maintained catalogue,
- calculate cost and selling price,
- send and revise quotations,
- convert an accepted quotation into a booking,
- and retain communication history against the commercial record.

## Long-term platform vision

Over time, the platform should become a full tenant-aware business suite with integrated sales, finance, operations, and reporting, while keeping the architecture as a modular monolith inside the existing pnpm workspace unless a stronger need emerges.
